Inspiration:

French history

Furina’s public persona is heavily inspired by French royalty, specifically Marie Antoinette. The overarching lore of Fontaine and Furina draws parallels to Biblical themes. Just as Christ sacrificed himself to wash away the sins of humanity, Furina sacrificed her divinity to save the people of Fontaine from their inherited, primordial sin.
